# Legal

This repository contains the publicly accessible legal policies, licenses, agreements, and other legal documentation produced for and used by **NullCloud**.

The purpose of this repository is to provide a central place for users, contributors, developers, and other interested parties to access the legal documents relevant to NullCloud and its projects.

## What's Included

This repository may contain:

* Policies and terms
* Software and source-code licenses
* Contributor agreements
* Intellectual property notices
* Third-party license information
* Other legal documents relevant to NullCloud

## Updates

Policies and legal documents may be updated from time to time as NullCloud's services, projects, or requirements change.

Whenever possible, the latest versions will be committed to this repository. Previous versions may remain available through the repository's commit history.

Documents may also include their own version numbers or effective dates where applicable.

## Notice

The documents in this repository are provided for reference. A specific project or service may have additional terms that apply to its use.

If there is a conflict between documents, the terms of the applicable and most recent agreement or policy should be consulted.
